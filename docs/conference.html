TomcatCon

TomcatCon is the place for all users of Tomcat to expand their Tomcat knowledge in areas such as networking, security, performance and deployment. It also offers an opportunity to discuss the current Tomcat roadmap and help inform future development.

TomcatCon Las Vegas

When

September 10 to 11, 2019.

Note: The main conference is September 09 to 12, 2019. We will have 2 days of Tomcat track.

Where

Flamingo Hotel, Las Vegas, Nevada, as part of ApacheCon North America 2019.

What

This is a two day track dedicated to Apache Tomcat on the Tueday and Wenesday of the main conference.

Registration for TomcatCon is open. Registration includes access to ApacheCon North America.

Schedule

The full schedule will be available soon. There are two days of great content covering like:

  • roadmap
  • security
  • networking (reverse proxying, load-balancing, clustering)
  • performance and monitoring

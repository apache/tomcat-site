TomcatCon

TomcatCon is the place for all users of Tomcat to expand their Tomcat knowledge in areas such as networking, security, performance and deployment. It also offers an opportunity to discuss the current Tomcat roadmap and help inform future development.

TomcatCon Montréal

When

September 24 to 25, 2018.

Note: The main conference is September 25 to 27, 2018. The Tomcat track starts the day before.

Where

Montréal Marriott Chateau Champlain, Montréal, Canada, as part of ApacheCon North America 2018.

What

This is a two day track dedicated to Apache Tomcat that starts the day before the main conference.

Registration for TomcatCon is open. Registration includes access to ApacheCon North America.

Schedule

The full schedule is now available. There are two days of great content covering:

  • roadmap
  • security
  • networking (reverse proxying, load-balancing, clustering)
  • performance and monitoring
